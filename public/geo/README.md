## Sea Ice Index citation

Fetterer, F., Knowles, K., Meier, W. N., Savoie, M., Windnagel, A. K. & Stafford, T. (2025). *Sea Ice Index* (G02135, Version 4) [Data Set]. National Snow and Ice Data Center. https://doi.org/10.7265/a98x-0f50. Retrieved 2026-08-31.

Two distinct official products are shown without conflation:

- **Areas:** observed monthly ice-extent polygons from the latest complete year available at retrieval, 2025. Global winter combines Northern Hemisphere March 2025 with Southern Hemisphere September 2025; global summer combines Northern Hemisphere September 2025 with Southern Hemisphere March 2025.
- **Lines:** 1981–2010 monthly median extent polylines for March and September, both hemispheres. These climatological edges are unchanged from the earlier build.

Both products are densified by 25 km in their native polar stereographic projection before WGS84 conversion, then grouped into global winter/summer observations. Source polygon topology is preserved: holes are not filled and no artificial polar closure is added. This is not current ice conditions or a navigation product.
